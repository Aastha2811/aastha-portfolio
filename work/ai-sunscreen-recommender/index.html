An AI scan that picks the right sunscreen

An AI face scan that recommends the right sunscreen, launched as part of a wider sunscreen push. It tripled scan participation, and then I had to keep its scan costs from running away.

What it is

A guided flow that ends in the cart, with several entry points across the app leading into the scan. Entry points went in once the second version of the AI scan was signed off.

  • Skin report scans grew too, so the jump wasn't only from adding a new scan type.
  • The sunscreen scan converted better: 3.99% vs 2.16% from onboarding to purchase in April 2026.
  • Scan participation settled at around 30–35%.

Problems caught and fixed

Scan credits burning too fast

The sunscreen scan used ~34% of all paid scan credits, with a far bigger sale-month surge than the skin report scan. Credits ran out in April and scan errors rose. I built a scan review dashboard and five Mixpanel alerts for credit burn and error spikes, flagged scans by logged-out users as wasted credits, and forecast that the remaining credits would last through August with ~28K scans to spare.

Scans were sale-driven, not a habit

The daily scan rate fell 58% after the sale, and normal-month scans were declining ~19% month on month. That shaped what came next.

What's next

  • Move the scan to a more visible spot and extend it into seasonal recommendations on other product pages.
  • A sunscreen retention journey built around a daily-use habit rather than visible results.
  • A sunscreen scan funnel in the retention MIS, to check whether scans turn into orders or only into credit cost.

Caveats

  • The 3.99% vs 2.16% comparison is between two different funnels and audiences, so treat it as directional.
  • ~85% of sunscreen scans happen in sale months, which inflates both volume and conversion.

Sources: Mixpanel, scan review dashboard, vendor credit records, Q1–Q2 roadmap.
