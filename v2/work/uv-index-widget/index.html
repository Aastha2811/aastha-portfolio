Using live UV levels to sell more sunscreen

Showing shoppers their live local UV Index on the sunscreen product page, so sun protection feels relevant right now. Two rounds of testing decided what it should look like and where it should sit.

What we built

A widget on the sunscreen PDP showing the shopper's local UV level. When the current reading was low or moderate, it showed the day's peak instead, so the message stayed relevant. It went live first on the three core sunscreens: Matte, Dewy and Glow.

Round 1: does visibility matter?

VariantWhat users sawConversion
ControlNo UV info1.40%
Variant 1UV widget, not sticky, with peak UV1.49%
Variant 2 · winnerSticky widget at the top, with peak UV1.65%

Adding the widget alone barely moved conversion. Making it sticky did: visibility and persistence are what make it work.

Round 2: a severity scale, and where it sits

VariantWhat users sawConversion
ControlWidget at the top, no scale1.18%
Variant 1Widget at the top, with scale1.55%
Variant 2 · shippedWidget above offers, with scale1.53%

The scale drove the lift. The two placements performed almost identically, so I shipped the one above offers: it puts the UV message right where purchase intent peaks, and works as a permanent slot without crowding the top of the page.

What I learned

  • A useful signal only works if people see it: sticky beat non-sticky.
  • A number needs context. The severity scale turned "UV 8" into something shoppers understood.
  • Placement near offers ties the message to the buying moment.
  • When two variants tie, pick the one that holds up as a permanent part of the page.

Caveats

  • The two rounds ran at different times with different controls, so compare variants within a round, not across rounds.
  • Sample sizes and significance weren't recorded in the pre-reads.

Sources: website pre-reads (12 and 26 Feb 2026), Q1 roadmap, Tinted sunscreen rollout.
